## 1. Visual Direction & Anti-Slop Rules
Build an authoritative, utilitarian B2B industrial equipment marketplace inspired by **McMaster-Carr**, **Machinio**, and **Linear**. 
- **NEVER use AI clichés:** No purple/indigo/cyan gradients, no glowing drop shadows, no glassmorphism, no floating 3D blobs, and no rounded bubbly "startup" cards.
- **Copywriting bans:** Never use buzzwords like "elevate," "unleash," "cutting-edge," "revolutionize," "seamless," or "next-gen." Use direct, plainspoken trade terminology (e.g., "Verified Inventory," "Max Sheet Size," "Impression Count," "Inspect & Quote").
